What's the Difference?

Our 501(c)(4) entity — SolarWAVE Action — is a social welfare organization that can engage in lobbying and direct political advocacy without restriction. Donations are not tax-deductible, but they directly fund the campaigns that move legislation.

Tax-Deductible Giving

Our affiliated 501(c)(3) — SolarWAVE Action Research & Education Project — supports non-partisan research, data tools, and public education. Contributions here are tax-deductible as charitable donations under IRS rules.
