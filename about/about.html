Who We Are

Built for Equitable Clean Energy.

SolarWAVE Action is an independent research and advocacy organization advancing clean energy through legal and data analysis. We build the tools, standards, and coalitions that make distributed clean energy ownership the default, not the exception.

Our People

In 2025, Angela Lipanovich partnered with Jenny Folkesson to support the passage of California Assembly Bill 1104, a commercial solar energy bill successfully signed into law in October that year. That collaboration became the foundation for SolarWAVE Action — built on the conviction that rigorous data analysis and sharp legal strategy are the most powerful tools for accelerating the clean energy transition.

Executive Director & Co-Founder
Jenny Folkesson

Jenny Folkesson is the Executive Director and co-founder of SolarWAVE Action.

She holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Copenhagen and has almost two decades of experience in data science, machine learning, and software engineering across academia, startups, and nonprofits. She spent most of that career in biotech, most recently at the Chan Zuckerberg Biohub, where she ran image analysis initiatives in the Data Science team.

Driven by a passion for using technology to make the world better, she brought those skills over to climate action, contributing data science work to Save Our Shores and Reef Check before she and Angela co-founded SolarWAVE Action.

Co-Founder & Board Chair
Angela Lipanovich

Angela Lipanovich is co-founder of SolarWAVE Action and founder and principal attorney of Estriatus Law, where she serves both as outside general counsel and project-specific advisor to entities and system owners across the cleantech and renewable energy spectrum — from early-stage startups and established public enterprises to private individuals and businesses.

She founded Estriatus Law not just to practice law, but to support the people and companies building a cleaner, smarter, more sustainable future. With every client, every project, and every partnership, she brings mission-driven legal guidance designed to clear obstacles and create momentum — because she believes the future depends on it.

Board Treasurer
Sarah Craft

Sarah Craft is a finance and business leader with 19 years of experience spanning financial management, strategic planning, operations, business analytics, and organizational growth. She currently serves as Vice President of Finance at SPK and Associates, a technology and engineering consulting firm.

An entrepreneur as well as a finance executive, Sarah was a co-founder and CFO of Santa Cruz Waves, a community-focused media company. She holds a B.S. in Health Sciences from UC Santa Cruz and an MBA from the McCombs School of Business at UT Austin, where she was inducted into the Beta Gamma Sigma Honor Society and selected as a McCombs+ Leadership Fellow.

Board Director
Rebecca Kaufman

Rebecca Kaufman is the founder and executive director of 5 Calls Civic Action, a national non-profit dedicated to making civic advocacy accessible to everyone. Since its launch in late 2016, 5 Calls has helped millions of people take direct action and make their voices heard by their elected officials.

Before turning to advocacy, Rebecca spent nearly two decades as a biomedical research scientist in academic and industry settings, where she developed a deep commitment to rigorous, evidence-based thinking.

Board Director
Pete Scudder

Pete, along with his wife Karen, founded Scudder Roofing out of his garage on the Monterey Peninsula in 1982. Today, the award-winning, multi-million dollar residential and commercial roofing company represents the highest standards in the industry, with more than 23,000 roofs installed.

In 2007, Pete founded sister company Scudder Solar Electrical Energy Systems — one of the leading solar energy companies in California — solidifying his place as an innovative business leader and important industry voice.

Board Director
Jan Freiwald

Jan Freiwald is the Executive Director of the Reef Check Foundation. Jan has deep expertise in reef community ecology, marine management, citizen science, and marine conservation. Prior to becoming Executive Director, Jan led the Reef Check California program for eight years, growing it into a statewide marine protected area monitoring network of citizen scientists.

He studied biology in Germany before receiving a B.S. in Marine Biology from UC Santa Cruz and a Ph.D. in Ecology and Evolutionary Biology from UC Santa Cruz.

Board Director
Kristen Brown

Kristen Brown is the Executive Director of the Santa Cruz Area Chamber of Commerce. She has 14+ years of experience serving the Santa Cruz County community as a congressional aide, government relations specialist, council member, mayor, and non-profit board member.

Having served eight years on the Capitola City Council including two terms as Mayor, Kristen was awarded the 2018 Santa Cruz County Women's Commission Trailblazer Award and named one of Silicon Valley Business Journal's “40 Under 40" in 2024.

Advisor
Galina Hale

Galina Hale is a Professor of Economics and Coastal Science and Policy, Faculty Director of the Institute for Social Transformation, and Co-Director of the Center for Analytical Finance at UC Santa Cruz. She is a Research Associate at the NBER and a Research Fellow at the CEPR.

Previously, she served as a Research Advisor at the Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco and as an assistant professor of economics at Yale University. Her research focuses on attracting mainstream finance to climate solutions and international financial stability.

Advisor

Barry Cinnamon is a long-time advocate of renewable energy and a widely recognized solar power and battery system expert. He oversees Cinnamon Energy Systems, a leading residential and commercial contractor in Silicon Valley, and hosts the weekly Energy Show podcast.

Barbara Alderete is an accomplished C-suite Executive Business Partner with more than 20 years of experience bringing operational clarity, strategic alignment, and leadership continuity to public, global, and high-growth organizations.
