Who We Are

Built for Equitable Clean Energy.

SolarWAVE Action is an independent research and advocacy organization advancing clean energy through data analysis, education, and evidence-based policy. We focus on transparency and practical solutions that strengthen California's clean energy future.

Mission

Our Mission & Vision

01 — Mission

Our Mission

To advance clean energy policy through data-driven research that is open, rigorous, and actionable.

02 — Vision

Our Vision

A future with equitable, resilient clean energy for all Californians — powered by policy that holds up to scrutiny.

How We Work

01 — Deliver

What We Deliver

Open methodologies, transparent data analysis, and actionable guidance — every assumption, dataset, and line of code published for public scrutiny.

02 — Translate

Policy Translation

We turn complex energy data into concise briefings for lawmakers and stakeholders — making evidence accessible to the people who shape policy.

03 — Engage

Public Accountability

We invite scrutiny and constructive debate through open data access — because clean energy policy that doesn't hold up to scrutiny won't hold up in Sacramento.

Our Founders

Executive Director & Co-Founder
Jenny Folkesson

Jenny Folkesson is the Executive Director and co-founder of SolarWAVE Action.

She holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Copenhagen and has almost two decades of experience in data science, machine learning, and software engineering — notably at the Chan Zuckerberg Biohub, where she led imaging initiatives. Driven by a passion for using technology to make the world better, she transitioned her expertise toward climate action, contributing data science work to Save Our Shores and Reef Check before she and Angela co-founded SolarWAVE Action.

Co-Founder
Angela Lipanovich

Angela Lipanovich is co-founder of SolarWAVE Action and founder and principal attorney of Estriatus Law, where she serves both as outside general counsel and project-specific advisor to entities across the cleantech and renewable energy spectrum — from early-stage startups to established public enterprises. Her clients range in size and structure, but share a common purpose: building technologies and solutions that drive a more sustainable world.

Before launching Estriatus Law, Ms. Lipanovich served as General Counsel for a publicly traded solar company, where she played a key role in lobbying to uncap the residential solar investment tax credit and supported the successful raise of over $44 million in capital.

She founded Estriatus Law not just to practice law, but to support the pioneers building a cleaner, smarter, more sustainable future. With every client, every project, and every partnership, she brings mission-driven legal guidance designed to clear obstacles and create momentum — because she believes the future depends on it.
