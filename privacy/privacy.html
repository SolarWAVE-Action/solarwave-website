Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Introduction

SolarWAVE Action ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website at solarwaveaction.org (the "Site") or interact with our organization.

Please read this policy carefully. By using our Site, you agree to the practices described here. If you have questions, contact us at hello@solarwaveaction.org.

Who We Are

SolarWAVE Action operates as a 501(c)(4) social welfare organization based in Santa Cruz, California. Our affiliated research and education activities are conducted under fiscal sponsorship through the American Solar Energy Society (ASES), a 501(c)(3) organization. References to "SolarWAVE Action" in this policy cover both our (c)(4) operations and our fiscally sponsored (c)(3) project unless otherwise noted.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Newsletter signups: When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address via Mailchimp.
  • Donation payments: When you make a donation, payment is processed by Stripe. We receive a record of the donation amount and your name but do not store your full payment card details — these are handled entirely by Stripe.
  • Research inquiry form: When you submit a commissioned research inquiry, we collect your name, email address, organization name, organization type, and the details of your inquiry.
  • Email correspondence: If you contact us directly by email, we retain that correspondence.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our Site, certain information is collected automatically, including:

  • Your IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on the Site
  • Referring URLs (how you arrived at our Site)

This information is collected through standard web analytics tools and server logs. We use this data to understand how our Site is used and to improve it.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send you our newsletter and research updates (with your consent)
  • Process and acknowledge donations
  • Respond to research inquiries and manage commissioned engagements
  • Understand how our Site is used and improve its content
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use Mailchimp (email), Stripe (payments), and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Each has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on our behalf.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our organization or others.
  • Organizational affiliates: We may share relevant information with our fiscal sponsor, ASES, as required for administrative and compliance purposes.

Third-Party Services

Our Site relies on the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy practices:

  • Mailchimp (newsletter) — mailchimp.com/legal/privacy
  • Stripe (donation processing) — stripe.com/privacy
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting) — docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies

We encourage you to review these policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

Cookies

Our Site may use cookies or similar tracking technologies to improve your experience. These may include:

  • Functional cookies: Required for basic site functionality
  • Analytics cookies: Used to understand Site usage (e.g. page views, traffic sources)

You can control cookie settings through your browser. Note that disabling cookies may affect Site functionality.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Delete your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for email communications at any time by clicking "Unsubscribe" in any email we send
  • Opt out of certain data collection practices

California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). [Note for Angela: please expand this section with full CCPA/CPRA disclosures as applicable.]

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@solarwaveaction.org.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Newsletter subscribers' data is retained until you unsubscribe. Donation records are retained as required for tax and accounting purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
