Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

1. Introduction

SolarWAVE Action ("we, us, or our") is a California nonprofit social welfare organization recognized under Section 501(c)(4) of the Internal Revenue Code. We are committed to protecting the privacy of visitors, supporters, donors, volunteers, partners, and members of the public (collectively, "you" or "your") who interact with our website at https://solarwaveaction.org/ (the "Site"), our blog and educational materials, our email lists, and our project inquiry and donation channels (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information we collect, how we use and share it, the choices and rights available to you, and how we safeguard your information. It is designed to comply with the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA/CPRA"), and—where applicable to visitors located in the European Economic Area ("EEA"), United Kingdom, or Switzerland—the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR").

Because we are a 501(c)(4) nonprofit, certain U.S. privacy laws (including the CCPA/CPRA) may not legally apply to us in all circumstances. Nonetheless, we have voluntarily adopted the consumer-friendly disclosures, rights, and practices described in this Policy as a matter of good governance and respect for your privacy.

By accessing or using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the Services.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to information collected through:

  • The Site and any subdomain, landing page, microsite, or campaign page operated by us;
  • Online forms used to submit project inquiries, partnership requests, volunteer interest, or other communications;
  • Email-list and newsletter sign-ups managed through our email service provider;
  • Donation pages hosted by or linking to our third-party payment processor (Stripe);
  • Our blog, educational materials, downloadable resources, and any associated comment, sharing, or feedback features; and
  • Our official pages and content on third-party social media platforms, only to the extent we control the data collection.

This Policy does not apply to third-party websites, applications, or services that we link to or that integrate with our Site. Those third parties are governed by their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

3. Information We Collect

We collect information from and about you in three general ways: (a) information you provide directly, (b) information collected automatically, and (c) information from third-party sources.

3.1 Information You Provide Directly. We collect the following types of information when you interact with us:

  • Inquiry and Contact Information. When you submit a project inquiry, partnership request, media request, volunteer form, or other contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number (if provided), organization or affiliation (if provided), the subject matter of your inquiry, and any other information you choose to share in the message.
  • Email-List and Newsletter Sign-Up. When you join our email list, we collect your email address and, where you provide it, your name, ZIP code, and topic preferences (e.g., clean energy, climate policy, environmental justice).
  • Donation Information. When you make a donation, our third-party payment processor (Stripe) collects your name, billing address, email address, payment card or bank-account information, and donation amount. We receive a record of your donation (including your name, email, donation amount, date, and any designation), but we do not receive or store full payment card numbers.
  • User-Generated Content. If you post comments on our blog, respond to a survey, submit testimonials, or upload materials with an inquiry, we collect that content along with any metadata you choose to share.
  • Event and Program Registration. If we host webinars, workshops, town halls, or trainings, we may collect registration details such as your name, email, and any accommodations requested.
  • Employment, Volunteer, and Internship Applications. If you apply to join us, we collect the information you submit in your application, including resume, references, and contact details.
  • Communications With Us. We retain copies of correspondence (including email, voicemail, and chat transcripts) so we can respond to you and maintain accurate records.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically. When you visit the Site, we and our service providers may automatically collect certain information about your device and your interaction with the Site, including:

  • IP address, approximate geolocation derived from IP, device identifiers, browser type and version, operating system, and language settings;
  • Pages and resources viewed, links clicked, time spent on pages, referring/exit URLs, search terms used to find the Site, and dates/times of access;
  • Information collected through cookies, pixels, software development kits (SDKs), web beacons, and similar tracking technologies (see Section 6); and
  • Aggregated metrics about engagement with our blog posts, educational materials, and downloadable resources.

3.3 Information From Third Parties. We may receive information about you from third parties, including:

  • Stripe (donation transaction confirmations, fraud-prevention signals, and limited payment metadata);
  • Mailchimp (or our then-current email service provider) (engagement metrics such as opens, clicks, bounces, unsubscribes, and inferred interests);
  • Google Analytics (aggregate and pseudonymous usage statistics about the Site);
  • Social media platforms (such as Meta/Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, YouTube), where you choose to interact with our content using their services or use a social-share or sign-in feature;
  • Co-sponsors and coalition partners with whom you have separately interacted and who share information with us in accordance with their own privacy policies; and
  • Publicly available sources, such as government databases, news outlets, and professional directories, when relevant to our advocacy and research mission.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes, consistent with our charitable and social-welfare mission:

  • To respond to your inquiries, project submissions, partnership proposals, volunteer interest, and other requests;
  • To process donations, send tax receipts and acknowledgments, and maintain donor records as required by law and good nonprofit practice;
  • To send you our newsletter, action alerts, advocacy campaigns, fundraising appeals, event invitations, and educational materials about clean energy, climate change, and social-justice issues;
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site and our Services, including diagnosing technical issues and analyzing usage trends;
  • To produce, publish, and distribute our blog, podcasts, videos, reports, white papers, toolkits, and other educational content, and to measure their reach and impact;
  • To organize and conduct events, trainings, town halls, public comment periods, and grassroots organizing activities;
  • To engage in lobbying, advocacy, voter education, and other social-welfare activities permitted under Section 501(c)(4), including communications about candidates, ballot measures, and public officials, in accordance with applicable federal and state law;
  • To recruit, evaluate, and onboard volunteers, interns, fellows, contractors, and employees;
  • To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, audit, tax, and reporting obligations (including IRS and state charitable-solicitation requirements);
  • To detect, prevent, investigate, and address security incidents, fraud, abuse, or violations of our terms; and
  • To establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We will not use your sensitive personal information (as defined under California law) for purposes other than those permitted by Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.121, and we will not retaliate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

5. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA/UK Visitors)

If you are located in the EEA, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we rely on the following legal bases under the GDPR/UK GDPR to process your personal data:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for marketing emails, non-essential cookies and tracking, and other situations where we ask for your express permission. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract / Pre-Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — to take steps at your request before responding to an inquiry, processing a donation, or fulfilling event registration.
  • Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — to comply with applicable laws, including donor-disclosure, tax, and lobbying-disclosure obligations.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — to operate and improve our Services, advance our public-interest mission, prevent fraud, and communicate with our supporters, balanced against your rights and interests.
  • Vital Interests (Art. 6(1)(d)) — in rare circumstances, to protect a person's life or physical safety.

Where we process special categories of personal data (e.g., information that may reveal political opinions disclosed when you tell us why you support our advocacy work), we rely on your explicit consent or another lawful condition under Article 9 GDPR, such as your manifest public disclosure or our processing in connection with a not-for-profit body with a political, philosophical, religious, or trade-union aim.

6. Cookies, Analytics, and Tracking Technologies

We and our service providers use cookies, pixels, web beacons, tags, and similar technologies (collectively, "Cookies") to operate the Site, remember your preferences, measure traffic, and (where permitted) personalize and measure communications.

6.1 Categories of Cookies We Use. We deploy cookies in the following categories:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies. Required for core Site functionality, such as security, network management, accessibility, and form submissions. These cannot be turned off in our systems.
  • Functional Cookies. Remember choices you make (such as language or region) and provide enhanced features.
  • Performance and Analytics Cookies. Help us understand how visitors interact with the Site through services such as Google Analytics. They collect information in an aggregated and (where supported) IP-anonymized form.
  • Advertising and Social Media Cookies/Pixels. Set by social media platforms (such as Meta/Facebook Pixel and the X/Twitter Pixel) and other third parties to measure the effectiveness of our outreach and to permit us to engage with our audience on those platforms. These technologies may share information with the platforms about your interaction with our content, even if you do not click on it. Where required by law, we will only deploy these technologies after you provide consent or after a sufficient cookie-banner choice.

6.2 Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics, a web-analytics service provided by Google LLC ("Google"), to understand how visitors use the Site. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar identifiers to collect information about your use of the Site, which is then aggregated with data from other users. You can learn more about Google's practices at policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

6.3 Email Platform (Mailchimp). We use Mailchimp (operated by Intuit Inc.) or a comparable email service provider to deliver our newsletters, action alerts, and event communications. Mailchimp uses tracking pixels and links to measure opens, clicks, deliverability, and engagement. You can unsubscribe from these emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email you receive from us, by emailing communications@solarwaveaction.org, or by managing your preferences in our preference center.

6.4 Social Media Pixels. Our Site uses pixels and SDKs from social media platforms, including Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and X (formerly Twitter), to measure the reach of our advocacy and educational content and to enable us to communicate with our audience on those platforms. These technologies may permit the platforms to recognize you across services and devices, including when you are logged into their services. We do not control these platforms' practices; please review their privacy policies for more information.

6.5 Your Choices About Tracking. You have several options for limiting or opting out of tracking:

  • Browser Settings. Most browsers let you delete or refuse Cookies. Disabling Cookies may affect Site functionality.
  • Cookie Banner. Where required, our cookie banner allows you to accept, reject, or customize non-essential Cookies.
  • Global Privacy Control (GPC). We treat GPC signals from supporting browsers as a valid request to opt out of the sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising on the browser, where the CCPA/CPRA applies.
  • Do Not Track (DNT). The Site does not currently respond to DNT browser signals because no consensus standard has emerged.
  • Platform Opt-Outs. You can adjust ad-personalization controls within Meta, X, Google, and other platforms to limit how they use your data.

Service providers are contractually limited to using personal information only for the purposes for which it was disclosed and consistent with this Policy and applicable law.

Consistent with our 501(c)(4) social-welfare mission, we may from time to time share information about supporters and donors with carefully selected coalition partners, allied advocacy organizations, or fiscal sponsors when doing so advances a shared advocacy or educational program. 7.4 Business Transfers. If SolarWAVE Action is involved in a merger, asset transfer, restructuring, dissolution, or similar transaction, personal information may be transferred to a successor entity, subject to commitments at least as protective as this Policy.

7.5 With Your Direction or Consent. We may share information with other parties at your direction or with your consent, such as when you ask us to introduce you to a partner organization or post a public comment.

7.6 Aggregated and De-Identified Information. We may share aggregated, anonymized, or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you for research, advocacy, education, fundraising, and reporting purposes.

8. Donations and Payment Processing

Donations are processed by Stripe, Inc., a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. When you donate:

  • Stripe collects and stores your payment-card or bank-account information directly. We do not see or store your full payment card number, CVV, or bank-account number on our servers.
  • We receive a record of the transaction (such as your name, email, donation amount, currency, date, transaction ID, and any designation).
  • Stripe's processing of your information is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy and applicable financial-services laws.
  • As a 501(c)(4) organization, donations to us are generally not tax-deductible as charitable contributions for federal income-tax purposes. We will provide a receipt and acknowledgement letter as a courtesy.
  • We retain donor records (including those of "major donors") in accordance with applicable IRS, state attorney general, and state charitable-solicitation requirements. Certain donor information may be required to be reported to the IRS on Schedule B of Form 990 or to state regulators; we treat such information as confidential to the maximum extent permitted by law.

Recurring donors may manage or cancel their recurring gifts at any time by contacting us at communications@solarwaveaction.org.

Our Site includes a blog and library of educational materials concerning clean energy, climate change, and social-justice issues. If you post a comment, submit a question, or otherwise interact with these resources, please remember that any information you make publicly available may be read, collected, and used by others. We are not responsible for the personal information you choose to disclose publicly.

11. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These include encryption in transit (TLS) for the Site and donation pages, restricted access on a need-to-know basis, multi-factor authentication for sensitive systems, vendor due diligence, and incident-response procedures.

No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information, and you provide it at your own risk. If we learn of a security incident affecting your personal information, we will notify you and applicable authorities as required by law.

12. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, comply with our legal, regulatory, tax, audit, and reporting obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Specifically:

  • Inquiry records: typically retained for up to three (3) years after our last substantive communication with you, unless a longer period is necessary;
  • Email-list subscribers: retained until you unsubscribe and for a reasonable suppression period thereafter;
  • Donor records: retained for at least seven (7) years to satisfy IRS, audit, and state charitable-solicitation requirements;
  • Website analytics data: retained per our Google Analytics configuration (typically 14 to 26 months);
  • Job applications and volunteer records: retained per applicable employment-records requirements; and
  • Backups: rotated and overwritten on routine schedules.

When we no longer need personal information, we will delete, destroy, or de-identify it in a secure manner.

15. Your Rights Under the EU and UK GDPR

If you are located in the EEA, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following rights with respect to your personal data, subject to applicable exemptions:

  • Access — obtain confirmation of whether we process personal data about you and request a copy.
  • Rectification — request that we correct or complete inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data in certain circumstances.
  • Restriction — request that we restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Portability — receive personal data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and have it transmitted to another controller, where technically feasible.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests, including profiling, and to direct marketing at any time.
  • Withdraw Consent — withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.
  • Lodge a Complaint — lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority, including the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) or your EEA supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information in Section 19. We will respond within one month and may extend that period where permitted by law.
