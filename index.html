About

We Turn Data
Into Policy.

California sits at the center of America's clean energy transformation — and at the center of its most contentious energy policy debates. SolarWAVE Action exists to ensure those debates are shaped by evidence and grounded in law.

We are a nonpartisan research and advocacy organization dedicated to advancing equitable, data-driven clean energy policy across California. Our team produces rigorous legal analysis coupled with open-source data analysis, then translates those findings into clear reports, legislative testimony, and policy recommendations that lawmakers and advocates can actually use.

We don't accept funding that constrains our conclusions. We publish every assumption, every dataset, and every line of code behind our work. Because clean energy policy that doesn't hold up to scrutiny won't hold up in Sacramento.

01 — Principle

Accountability

Every publication includes our software, data sources, and legal resources — open to anyone who wants to check our work.

02 — Principle

Independence

We do not accept funding that constrains our research conclusions. Our findings go where the evidence leads.

03 — Principle

Equitability

A clean energy future that doesn't serve all Californians isn't worth advocating for. Equity is embedded in every policy recommendation we make.

What We Do

01 — Analysis

Open-Source Research

We perform transparent data analysis using open-source software and credible public datasets — publishing every model and methodology so our work can be independently verified.

02 — Education

Clear Communication

We publish reports, articles, and data visualizations designed for policymakers, advocates, and the public — translating complex energy economics into accessible, actionable insight.

03 — Advocacy

Evidence-Based Policy

We bring our research directly into the policy arena — engaging lawmakers, regulators, and stakeholders with concrete proposals to safeguard and expand California's clean energy infrastructure.
